Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

Chat Went Off ("we") runs an automated service that makes short, credited clips of public live streams and posts them to our own YouTube channel and Instagram account, together with clips of footage that brands and creators provide for that purpose.

What we process

  • Public stream information from the official Twitch, Kick and YouTube APIs: channel names, stream titles, categories, viewer counts and public clip metadata.
  • Chat activity as numbers only: how many messages and emotes per second. We never store the text of chat messages or who sent them.
  • Short video segments of public streams, used to make a clip and deleted within 24 hours.
  • Performance of our own posts (views, likes, watch time) from YouTube Analytics and Instagram Insights.

Google user data

We use Google OAuth only to upload videos to, and read analytics for, our own YouTube channel. We do not access any other Google account, and we do not share Google user data with anyone. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell or share personal data. Raw stream segments are deleted within 24 hours; rendered clips are deleted from our servers 7 days after posting. Operational logs are kept for up to 90 days.

Removal requests

Streamers can ask us to remove their clips and stop clipping them at chatwentoff.optout@gmail.com.
